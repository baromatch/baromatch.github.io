How Baromatch works

When the app opens, you’ll be able to:

  1. Pick your match Tonight or later in the week, with the kick-off time.
  2. Find your bar The ones showing it near you.
  3. Hold your spot For you or your whole group, before you head out. It won’t cost a thing.
  4. Arrive, and keep the night Scan in at the door so the bar knows you’re there. Afterwards, the night stays in your album as a coaster.

Get an email when the app opens in Paris

We only write when Baromatch opens in Paris, and about early tests. What we keep, and for how long (in French).

  • Who: Baromatch, and only Baromatch.
  • Why: to email you when the app opens in Paris, and about early tests.
  • What: your email address, and Paris as your city. Nothing else.
  • On what basis: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Who handles it: sign-ups go through Formspree, Inc., a provider based in the USA, which forwards them to our inbox. Your data is never sold or rented.
  • How long: until the app launches in Paris, then 12 months at most.
  • Your rights: to see, correct or delete your data, or to leave the list, write to contact@baromatch.fr from the address you signed up with. We delete it within a month. You can also complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority.
